LEAGUE RULES
Last updated: [DATE]


1. THE BASICS
Every week you pick five NFL games, straight up - no spreads, no point totals.
You score the points your teams score.


2. SCORING
- A correct pick scores the number of points that team scored.
- A wrong pick scores 0.
- A tie scores 0 and counts as a loss on your record. You didn't pick a winner.

Example: you pick Detroit, they win 38-10. You score 38 points.

3. PICK ORDER MATTERS
Rank your five picks 1 through 5. Order is the weekly tiebreaker (rule 6) and it
decides the jackpot (rule 9). Put your highest-scoring expectation at Pick 1.

4. DEADLINES
- Games that kick off before Sunday 1:00 PM ET - Thursday, Friday, Saturday,
  Sunday morning - lock at their own kickoff.
- Everything else - the Sunday 1:00 slate, the 4:25 window, Sunday night and
  Monday night - locks at Sunday 1:00 PM ET.

Once the 1:00 games start, your slate is frozen for the week. You cannot add,
change or reorder anything after that, including the Monday night game.

5. INCOMPLETE SLATES
You can submit fewer than five picks. Whatever you have at the lock is what
counts; empty slots score 0. There is no penalty beyond the points you give up.

6. WEEKLY WINNER
Highest total points for the week. Ties break by Pick 1 points, then Pick 2, and
so on down to Pick 5.

7. SEASON STANDINGS
Your overall total is the sum of your weekly totals. Season ties break by number
of weeks won.

8. VISIBILITY
Nobody can see anyone else's picks until the Sunday 1:00 lock. Before then the
leaderboard shows a lock icon for picks that are in but hidden, so you can see
who has submitted without seeing what they took. Once the lock passes,
everyone's full slate is visible - including Sunday night and Monday night.

9. BUY-IN AND PAYOUTS
Buy-in $180 ($10 per week). Full amount due prior to the start of the season

Weekly Payouts:
1st Place - 50% of the weekly pot
2nd Place - 25% of the weekly pot
3rd Place - 15% of the weekly pot
10% of the weekly pot goes to year end payouts 

End of Season Payouts: 
1st overall in points - 50% of the year end pot
2nd overall in points - 30% of the year end pot
3rd overall in points - 20% of the year end pot

10. SCHEDULE CHANGES (FLEX)
If the NFL moves a game, your pick moves with it automatically - you don't need
to do anything. The deadline still follows rule 4, so a game flexed into Sunday
night still locks at 1:00 PM, and a game flexed to Thursday locks at its new
kickoff. Check your picks when a flex is announced.

11. SCORES AND CORRECTIONS
Scores come from ESPN automatically. If ESPN is wrong or late, the commissioner
can correct a score by hand. Every correction is recorded with who made it, what
it was before, and when - and that history is visible to the commissioner.

12. DISPUTES
The commissioner has the final say.

Every pick change is logged with a timestamp. After a week is completely
finished, the commissioner can see every change made that week, so a question
like "I definitely picked the other team" can actually be checked.
